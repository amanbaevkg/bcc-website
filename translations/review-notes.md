# Translation review notes

Items for BCC (and a native speaker, especially for Luxembourgish) to confirm before launch.
No facts were added in translation; where the English source was ambiguous, the choice is listed here.

## Kept in the original language on purpose

- Testimonials: shown word for word in English on every language version (marked `lang="en"`).
- Business names, the address, “Bloc C” (the sign on the building).

## Choices to confirm

- **Slogan “Building tomorrow’s now”** (home): translated as FR “Bâtir demain dès aujourd’hui”,
  DE “Wir bauen das Morgen schon heute”, LU “Mir bauen de Muer elo schonn”. Creative line: confirm or
  keep English.
- **“Choose your ‘player’”** (services): the English wordplay is kept with the word “player” in FR/DE/LU.
- **“Reception & Switchboard”**: the live site says “Home & Switchboard”, a literal translation of the
  French “Accueil”; rendered as reception.
- **“Mail room”** service: the live site says “Email room”; the text describes mail handling.
- **Accounting, Legal & HR**: “Legal” rendered as FR “juridique”, DE “Recht”, LU “Juristesches”.
- **Luxembourgish** overall: written in standard Luxembourgish spelling; a native-speaker read-through
  is recommended.
